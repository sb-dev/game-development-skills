The selected set deliberately differs from the core and platformer selections. It prioritises action/system analysis plus a concrete action/state handoff, while retaining spatial, player-evidence and integrated-production perspectives. The opportunity cost is less dedicated comparative game-theory/luck/skill depth. Nystrom must earn its role through a gameplay-facing translation in P3; merely recommending classes or a state machine would not satisfy it. Fullerton/Lemarchand overlap is accepted for their different evidence/integration roles, not counted as independent validation.

## 4. Source-reuse, permission and gap decisions

`TT-CORPUS-01` is the first tactical-pack corpus. The accepted catalogue explicitly kept the five supplied books in the **core** corpus without assigning any mandatory pack corpus. This decision fills new tactical slots; it does not remove or demote Swink from either already accepted corpus. All supplied core works remain available and unchanged. No user-mandated tactical title is excluded, no supplied file is altered, and no substitution approval is pending. The new Nystrom selection uses an empty tactical slot under the canonical selection rule. Future changes to an established corpus must preserve decision history and any applicable supplied-book permissions.

Review of source evidence for selection is not completion of P3. P3 must verify the intended tactical applicability of all five and add direct examination where needed. No selected-source access gap remains. P4 must challenge information previews versus intentional uncertainty, cost/turn order and strategy coverage, optional cancellation/undo, input/UI access, opponent knowledge, and asynchronous presentation versus committed state. The initial scope stays bounded local tactical encounters, not networked synchronisation or proof of universally balanced play.
