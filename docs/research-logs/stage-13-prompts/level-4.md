# Level 4: three scale, interaction and repair designs

**State:** Designed, not executed. These are bounded game-specific proofs after the local core vertical. Seed checks, simulation and deliberately impaired delivery schedules must remain labelled; no internet-scale, human-equivalent or real-world simulation claim follows.

## E10 — Seeded Vault

**Production question:** Does generated progression remain playable after keys, gates, hazards and resources are placed, across a retained seed set?  
**Distinct evidence:** Procedural encounter composition, adverse strategies and post-placement reachability at scale.  
**Fault / preservation:** A key is placed behind its dependent gate; repair the placement/dependency constraint while preserving accepted combat, movement, reward and already-valid seeds.

```text
Build Seeded Vault in a fresh local browser project using installed game-development and game-evaluate with no selected Extension Pack. Make an original small roguelite run of three generated rooms with discrete movement, a limited resource, a key/gate dependency, hazards or simple declared opponents, optional reward and a final exit. Each run has complete success/failure/restart and explicit within-run versus retained progression. Keep the generator and runtime specific to this game, using ordinary existing tools rather than a general procedural engine.

Choose and record room-construction, placement, movement/action costs, opponent knowledge, resource/reward, progression and reset rules. State how a generated room can satisfy its objective under those actual actions and resources. Use a fixed evaluation seed set 1 through 20, retaining configuration and all failing seeds; do not discard a failing seed and count its replacement as its repair. Verify constraints after final key/gate/hazard/resource placement, not only after drawing connected floor tiles. Identify required and optional paths and collect witnesses or contrary traces under the actual game rules.

Run the resulting game, exercise real browser input and full runs, and distinguish same-runtime automated state/action checks from privileged setup or an independent model. Compare direct-exit, reward-seeking and resource-conserving policies with declared knowledge and limitations across the seed set. Inspect whether an apparent dominant strategy depends on omitted hazards, resource timing or invalid information. Preserve unsuccessful runs and do not infer human fun/depth from scripts. Provide keyboard-operable play/menus and readable critical cues without colour or sound dependence.

Measure generation and representative runtime load for the identified build: target each of the 20 small three-room generations below 500 ms, and p95 frame interval at most 33.3 ms during a recorded 60-second 1280 by 720 run after loading. Record actual workload, spikes and overhead; these are project targets, not universal thresholds. Keep larger-scale claims excluded.

After accepting a valid baseline, create a disclosed disposable seed/placement fault with a required key behind its gate. Reproduce the unsatisfied dependency, repair the responsible placement constraint, rerun that seed and the full fixed seed set plus affected complete runs. Preserve accepted rules and unchanged valid content where possible; changing combat/resource rules or bypassing the gate is not a causal repair. Retain baseline/fault/repaired generators, seeds and outputs.

Deliver the complete prompt, playable source, exact dependency/build/run commands, generation/progression contract, seed/policy data, actual tests/load evidence and captures with limitations. No invented human observations, paid services, accounts or external publication.
```

## E11 — Canal Works

**Production question:** Do competing flows, demand, congestion and saved progression stay consistent across scenarios and representative load?  
**Distinct evidence:** Systemic content, economy/strategy interactions, collection validity and persistent-state recovery.  
**Fault / preservation:** A saved facility/dependency reference restores an impossible mixed state after an interrupted write; fix serialization/reconstruction/recovery at its actual boundary, preserving economic rules and prior valid saves.

```text
Create Canal Works as an original small local browser management simulation using installed game-development and game-evaluate, with no pack selected. Build three authored connected canal scenarios with producers, routes, storage and delivery sinks. The player places or adjusts a small set of gates/links to route discrete cargo while handling capacity, demand and congestion. Each scenario has a bounded turn horizon, visible objective, completion/failure/retry and progression to the next scenario. Provide save/load of accepted progress and a documented reset policy. This is a game model, not a claim about real water or logistics.

Choose and record exact flow/update order, production/consumption, storage/capacity, building costs, demand, failure and progression rules. Make available actions and state readable in a keyboard-operable interface with pointer alternatives, visible focus and non-colour status meaning. Separate intended scarcity from invalid stock, duplication or unreachable mandatory delivery. Compare throughput-first, reserve-first and congestion-avoidance policies across all three scenarios; retain starting conditions, knowledge, trajectories and outcomes. Question apparent balance improvements if logs mix scenarios/retries or omit stalled cargo.

Implement actual browser play and small rule/flow/save tests. Validate collection against known actions and conservation rules defined by this game's sources/sinks, not a generic score. Exercise normal save/reload, a missing dependent object, corrupt/incompatible data and a deliberately interrupted write. Declare a recovery policy before running: restore a complete last-valid checkpoint or a clearly reported clean start, never a silent hybrid state. Use the selected runtime's normal storage and a bounded game-specific implementation, not a new persistence framework.

Profile representative scenarios at 64 and 256 active cargo objects on the identified runtime. Target update-step p95 at most 50 ms at the larger workload and report main-thread/render stalls separately during a 60-second interaction run; list exact workloads, sampling and limits. Do not infer huge-world support from this scale.

Keep an accepted baseline and a disclosed disposable faulty save/reconstruction variant causing an invalid facility/link relationship under interrupted persistence. Preserve failing data, diagnose storage versus reconstruction versus schema responsibility, and repair the smallest adequate unit. Rerun corrupt/interrupted/normal saves, all accepted scenario outcomes, resource invariants and affected load checks. Preserve costs, demand/order rules, unaffected UI/content and valid earlier save identities according to the declared compatibility policy.

Deliver playable source, complete prompt, exact local dependency/run commands, system/scenario/save contracts, actual policy/telemetry/test/profile results and baseline/fault/repair records. Do not invent player experience or causal conclusions from scripted logs. No account, remote service, paid dependency or external publication.
```

## E12 — Twin Signal

**Production question:** Can two actual clients coordinate across two rooms while authority, reward identity and reconnect recovery remain correct under declared adverse delivery?  
**Distinct evidence:** Real multi-peer execution, systemic cooperation, duplicate/drop/delay and session boundaries.  
**Fault / preservation:** A replayed accepted request pays a reward twice across disconnect; repair authoritative outcome identity rather than weakening the objective or disabling reconnection.

```text
Build Twin Signal as an original bounded local cooperative browser game using installed game-development and game-evaluate, with no Extension Pack selected. Use two separate browser clients connected through a real local authoritative server and a suitable existing transport library with recorded version. Do not represent multiplayer as two cursors inside one runtime, and do not create a general networking framework or hosted service. Make two connected rooms where each player's position/held signal affects a gate and the pair must move a shared parcel to an exit for a single team reward. Include failure, session restart and a clear disconnect/rejoin path.

Choose and record player input/position rules, signal/gate/parcel dependencies, collision, reward identity, server authority, message/session identity and join/leave/recovery policy before tests. State what each client may predict or merely display, and when authoritative state is expected to converge. Preserve distinct player inputs and readable own/partner/session information; support keyboard play, visible focus, pause/wait messages and non-colour/non-audio critical cues. Establish complete cooperative success, failure and two-room reset through real client input.

Run actual server/client tests at baseline delivery and controlled 100 ms and 250 ms application-message delays, including duplicated requests, one dropped application message, reconnect during resolution and a repeated request after reconnect. Label these as deliberately scheduled delivery conditions, not measurements of real packet loss or public internet behaviour. Retain both client observations and authoritative traces. After delivery settles, require the declared shared outcome to converge within 2 seconds; reward is issued once for the same accepted outcome. Do not require transient client equality where the architecture permits prediction. Inspect backlog/work under a recorded 60-second two-client workload and report processing/render measurements and limits.

Compare two cooperative strategies and inspect opportunity/fairness assumptions under the declared role information. Run a real two-person session about partner state and recovery instructions with recorded assistance; scripts cannot supply that human finding. If participants or actual multi-peer execution are unavailable, name the exact blocked proof rather than simulating success.

Retain the working baseline, introduce a disclosed duplicate-reward variant spanning reconnect, reproduce it and repair authoritative request/outcome handling. Preserve gate/parcel rules, valid new rewards, input roles and declared recovery. Rerun duplicate/drop/delay/rejoin and normal two-room outcomes; retain baseline/fault/repair outputs and unsuccessful traces.

Provide complete source, prompt, transport dependencies, exact local server/client/build/test commands, authority/session contract, actual multi-peer/human/load evidence and limitations. No accounts, paid hosting, production service/security certification or external publication.
```
